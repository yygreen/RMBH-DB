How we will do it

One $65,000 engagement. Four workstreams. 500 deliverables.

The budget is not split goal by goal. It is one integrated engagement. Every workstream supports more than one goal, and authority work in one area lifts all five. A "deliverable" is anything that ships to the live web — a page edit, a new article, a video, an audio piece, a published link, a schema or title-tag change, a new keyword target. The engagement commits to 500 deliverables, openly tracked.

Protect and optimize the current base

  • Lock the current priority keyword list.
  • Refresh the highest-value existing pages.
  • Fix internal links, titles, schema, and conversion paths.
  • Build weekly ranking alerts for declines.

Build the philanthropy donor layer

  • Create pages for legacy, impact, Israel charity, and poverty relief.
  • Develop content for donors giving from values, not emergency need.
  • Track general-market rankings separately from RMBH rankings.

Compete in the Israel charity category

  • Map Yad Eliezer and other top competitors.
  • Create donor-research pages around Israel, poverty, families, and holidays.
  • Pursue list placements, mentions, and relevant authority links.
  • Track share of organic traffic against the competitive set.

Build AI-recognized authority

  • Create source-rich pages AI platforms can cite.
  • Publish and optimize videos for Google and Gemini discovery.
  • Standardize entity signals across the website and outside profiles.
  • Run monthly AI prompt tests with screenshots and source notes.
